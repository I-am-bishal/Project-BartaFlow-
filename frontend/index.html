BartaFlow is inspired by the Bengali and Hindi word "বার্তা" (Barta), meaning message or news. Combined with Flow — representing seamless, unstoppable automation — the name captures our core promise: making every business message flow intelligently and automatically.

It's a name rooted in Indian culture, built for a global platform. Every WhatsApp message your customers send is a barta — and BartaFlow makes sure it's answered perfectly, every time.
